The mostexpensive fifteenminutes in a factory

Every month a utility meter keeps one number: the building's highest 15-minute demand. For a Massachusetts manufacturer that single interval can set a third of the bill or more. A Powerblock exists to lower it. I built an instrument that finds the sites where it can, prices each one under real 2026 tariffs, and runs the battery without knowing the future.

Built by Aditya Swaroop for Powertown's Machine Learning Engineer intern application, October 2026. Everything below runs in your browser on public data; the code and every number's source are linked at the end.

What is one Powerblock worth at this site?

Pick a site, or drop in your own interval data. The engine replays a full year at 15-minute resolution against Powertown's published Powerblock spec (250 kW, 522 kWh, LFP) and the tariff's exact billing rule, then prices the bill with and without the battery. Nothing leaves your browser.
